  • The Russian word involved, if the issue relates to dictionary data or search
  • A screenshot, if applicable

About Lexirus

Lexirus is an offline Russian-Chinese dictionary designed for Russian language learners. Its main features include dictionary lookup, word forms and conjugations, stress marks, example sentences, related words, notes, and customizable fuzzy search options.

No account or login is required to use the core dictionary features.

Please send only information needed to explain the issue and redact sensitive details in screenshots. You do not need to send your complete personal-data backup. Handling of voluntary support messages is described in our Privacy Policy.

Dictionary sources and permission

Lexirus includes BKRS dictionary data from bkrs.info. The source export is identified locally as August 30, 2026. The official download page permits free use of its databases for any purpose. Lexirus uses this published permission to integrate the data into its local dictionary. Rights in the original content remain with their respective holders.

Lexirus prepares local indexes, compressed storage and display corrections. These changes are maintained by Lexirus; the original source and contributors retain their respective rights. Lexirus is independently developed.

Some language data and corrections use OpenRussian and Russian Wiktionary under the CC BY-SA license applicable to the source version. Attribution, license and modification notices are retained in the third-party notices supplied with the App. Open-source components and fonts retain their own license notices.

For a source attribution or rights issue, please email xzwdev@163.com with the affected entry or material and relevant details so that it can be checked and addressed.

Original study materials and examination references

The original study notes and explanations in Lexirus are written and maintained by the developer. Russian major Level 8 examination materials are collected, organized and proofread by the developer. Rights in the original examination questions and any quoted content remain with their respective rights holders.

关于 Lexirus

Lexirus 是一款面向俄语学习者的离线俄汉词典。 主要功能包括词典查询、词形与变格变位、 重音标记、例句、关联词、笔记以及可自定义的模糊搜索功能。

使用主要词典功能无需注册或登录。

请只提供排查问题所需的信息，并遮盖截图中的敏感内容；无需发送完整个人资料备份。主动邮件反馈的信息处理方式见隐私政策。
