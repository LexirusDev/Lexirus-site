Lexirus Privacy Policy

A Simple and Practical Russian-Chinese Dictionary

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Local dictionary use and personal records

Lexirus works locally for dictionary lookup, word forms and fuzzy search. No Lexirus account or sign-in is required. Favorites, vocabulary lists, history, notes and preferences are stored on your device. The App does not automatically send these records, recordings or speech transcripts to developer servers, and has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. Retention, deletion and backups

You can edit or remove personal records in the App. Records remain on your device until you change or remove them, or erase the local database. For backup import and data merging, the local database and exported backups may retain deletion markers, the latest history-clear marker and note versions. Clearing history does not erase backup files you previously exported.

Exported backups contain personal records and note versions. You choose where to save them and are responsible for protecting and deleting copies. On iOS/iPadOS, offloading an app may keep documents and data; deleting the app removes its local app data. On macOS, removing the app may leave its local container, which must be removed separately if you want to erase its data. System/device backups follow your Apple settings and platform rules; Lexirus cannot guarantee deletion of those copies.

3. Voice input and read-aloud

Microphone and speech-recognition permissions are requested when you choose voice input. Russian speech search works only when on-device recognition is supported. Audio is used for that on-device session and is not saved or uploaded by Lexirus; the App does not fall back to server recognition. Read-aloud uses Apple's system text-to-speech service. You can revoke permissions in system settings; text lookup remains available.

4. Voluntary support messages

If you email the developer, your email address and the message, screenshots or device details you choose to include will be used to reply and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive data. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request; you can ask for deletion by email. It is not used for advertising or sold. External webpages and email services may handle information under their own policies when you choose to use them.

5. iCloud and policy updates

The current version does not provide iCloud synchronization of personal records. System iCloud backups, if enabled in your Apple settings, are separate from App synchronization. Before introducing optional iCloud synchronization, we will update this policy to describe which records are transferred, where they are stored and how users manage or delete them. This page will also be updated when other data practices change.

Lexirus 隐私政策

简洁实用的俄汉词典

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

1. 本地查词与个人记录

Lexirus 的查词、词形与模糊搜索在设备本地完成，无需注册或登录 Lexirus 账号。收藏、生词本、历史、笔记和偏好设置保存在设备本地。本应用不会自动向开发者服务器发送这些记录、录音或语音转写文本，不含广告、追踪或第三方统计分析 SDK。

2. 数据保留、删除与备份

你可以在应用内修改或删除个人记录；记录会保留在本地，直到你修改、删除相关记录或清除本地资料库。为支持备份导入与资料合并，本地资料库和导出的备份可能保留删除标记、最近的历史清空标记及笔记版本。清空历史不会删除此前已导出的备份文件。

导出备份包含个人记录和笔记版本，保存位置由你选择，请妥善保管并自行删除不再需要的副本。iOS/iPadOS 的“卸载 App”可能保留文稿与数据，“删除 App”会移除本机应用数据。Mac 删除应用后，本地容器可能仍保留；如果希望清除这些数据，需要同时删除该容器。系统及设备备份由你的 Apple 设置和平台规则管理，Lexirus 无法保证清除其中的副本。

3. 语音输入与朗读

只有你主动使用语音输入时才会请求麦克风及语音识别权限。俄语语音搜索仅在设备支持设备端识别时可用；音频仅用于当次本地识别，Lexirus 不保存或上传，也不会改用服务器识别。朗读由 Apple 系统语音服务提供。你可以在系统设置中撤销权限，文字查词仍可使用。

4. 主动联系与问题反馈

如果你主动发邮件反馈，开发者会使用你的邮箱及你选择提供的文字、截图或设备信息来回复和排查问题。请勿发送密码或不必要的敏感资料。反馈邮件仅在处理问题所需的时间内保留，你可以发邮件要求删除；这些信息不会用于广告或出售。你主动访问外部网页或使用邮件服务时，相关服务方可能按其自身政策处理信息。

5. iCloud 与政策更新

当前版本尚未提供个人记录的 iCloud 同步。你在 Apple 设置中启用的系统 iCloud 备份与本应用的同步功能不同。如果后续提供可选 iCloud 同步，我们会在启用前更新政策，说明上传哪些记录、存储位置及管理和删除方式。其他数据处理方式发生变化时，也会更新本页面。
